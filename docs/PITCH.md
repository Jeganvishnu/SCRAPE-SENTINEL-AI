# Scrape Sentinel AI — Hackathon Pitches

## 30-Second Elevator Pitch
"Web scrapers break silently whenever target websites change their layout, causing data corruption across downstream AI pipelines. Scrape Sentinel AI solves this. Powered by Bright Data Scraper Studio, our platform automatically detects extraction failures, uses AI to diagnose structural DOM changes with evidence, evaluates safe repairs through a Safety Gate, executes approved fixes through a self-healing engine, and only declares recovery after independent validation succeeds."

## 60-Second Hackathon Pitch
"Every web data pipeline faces a fundamental flaw: when target websites alter their HTML structure or CSS selectors, traditional scrapers fail silently or crash without telemetry.

Scrape Sentinel AI turns scraper failure into an observable, self-healing lifecycle. Built on Bright Data Scraper Studio (Collector `c_mt46lngz2asqzj8tkj`), our platform collects structured public data. When layout shifts occur, our Validation Engine detects missing fields.

Instead of letting AI blindly modify code, AI proposes explainable repairs backed by evidence from historical runs. Our Safety Gate evaluates risk policy—allowing low-risk repairs while flagging ambiguous changes for human review.

The Phase 5 self-healing engine re-runs extraction using the SAME Bright Data Collector ID, and recovery is only declared verified when independent validation passes 100%. All telemetry, MTTR, and health scores are tracked live on our observability dashboard."
